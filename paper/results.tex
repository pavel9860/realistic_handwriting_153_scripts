\paragraph{Results.} Table~\ref{tab:systems}: the searched bank closes 71\,\% of the embedding-FD gap between font rendering and real handwriting and raises precision from 0.10 to 0.14. The fitted personas reach FD 0.129 and precision 0.12. Without the scan model FD rises to 0.242 and recall falls to 0.001, while realism, which treats scanner noise as atypical of the clean IAM scans, moves up. Removing variation, joins or the pen model lowers both realism and precision; without joins the embedding FD is slightly lower, so FD alone does not rank these ablations. Table~\ref{tab:styles}: movement energy rises with effort from medical to calligraphic writing, entropy with it; accurate writing is the most legible for Tesseract and medical writing the least. Table~\ref{tab:dist}: the search makes the types much easier to tell apart while individuals stay identifiable.
